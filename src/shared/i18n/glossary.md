# zh-CN glossary

Use these terms consistently. Tone: concise, neutral, developer-tool register; no honorifics (use 你/你的 sparingly, usually omit the subject). Keep Latin product names as-is and put a space between Latin words and Chinese characters (`Natsu 界面`). Use full-width Chinese punctuation in Chinese sentences, “ ” for quotes.

## Terms kept in English

`MCP`, `Agent` / `Agents`, `Skill` / `Skills`, `Mono`, `CLI`, `SSH`, `Git` and provider names are never translated. Use the plural (`Agents`, `Skills`) only for the Settings section and rail names; in running text use the singular. Put a space between these words and adjacent Chinese (`添加 Skill`, `运行中的 Agent`).
